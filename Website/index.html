Common triggers for action

  • Record Keeping. AI is in the firm, but are all staff on the same page.
  • Court practice notes on AI. Australian courts have issued robust GenAI guidance for practitioners.
  • A client complaint or near-miss. Confidential data passed to an AI tool without a policy in place.
  • Shadow IT discovered. Staff running AI subscriptions the firm didn't know about.
The problem

Most firms are now using AI in some form.
Few have a documented position on it.

Australian courts have issued GenAI practice notes. PI insurers are increasingly asking about AI governance. And staff are adding tools that sometimes touch client data without anyone upstairs knowing. The risk is real, and it often surfaces without warning.
